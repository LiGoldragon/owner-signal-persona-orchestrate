# Upgrades

## 0.12.0 — Ethos-zero WireContract

This is a clean breaking wire deployment. The contract now has interface
version 0.2.0 and channel revision 5, and is generated by Ethos-zero 0.5.0
from committed `ethos/signal.ethos`; `build.rs`, ethos-monolith, signal-frame,
Dotos, and their legacy pins are removed. The hand-owned `codec.rs` is only
the validated, length-prefixed rkyv envelope around the generated frame.

Consumers must use the generated `Request`, `Reply`, and `Refusal` roots.
`ConfigurationRejected` is now a `Refusal` variant rather than a reply variant.
There is no compatibility decoder.

## 0.11.0 — default-store configuration

This breaks WireRevision 3. Upgrade every consumer with this contract before
deploying it. `Configure` now carries only the ordinary and meta socket paths:
the owning Orchestrate Nexus derives its Sema store path locally. The former
store-path field and `StorePathImmutable` refusal are removed, with no
compatibility decoder or migration.
# 1.0.0 to 1.1.0

The privileged contract uses the ordinary contract's shared Nexus
configuration, reports the persistent lifecycle receipt, and adds the sole
operation that reopens ordinary configuration.
